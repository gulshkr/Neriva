## Warning Signs: When to Seek Medical Attention
While mild stiffness can often improve with gentle mobility, certain warning signs require professional medical evaluation:
- Numbness, tingling, or 'pins and needles' sensations traveling down the limb.
- Persistent pain that awakens you from sleep at night.
- Noticeable joint swelling, warmth, or redness.
- Sudden loss of balance, muscle weakness, or inability to bear weight.
